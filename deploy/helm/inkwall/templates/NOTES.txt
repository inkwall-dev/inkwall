Inkwall {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Wait for the operator:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deployment/{{ include "inkwall.operator.name" . }}

Then protect a route with a WAFPolicy (examples: operator/config/samples in
the repository) and list the Inkwall objects:

  kubectl get inkwall -A

The operator validates rule sets and policies when they are saved, builds
them into a signed bundle, and wires every Ingress, IngressRoute and
HTTPRoute a WAFPolicy targets to the engine:

- Traefik (Ingresses, IngressRoutes, HTTPRoutes): the engine is injected
  into Traefik's pods when they start. Traefik pods that ran before the
  install are injected when restarted:

    kubectl -n <traefik namespace> rollout restart deployment/<traefik>

- Envoy Gateway (HTTPRoutes): the engine is added to the Envoy pods of the
  route's Gateway, which Envoy Gateway rolls itself.

Only proxy pods in these namespaces get the engine (injection.proxyNamespaces):

  {{ include "inkwall.proxyNamespaces" . }}

If Traefik or Envoy Gateway runs in another namespace, add it to
injection.proxyNamespaces: its routes are not wired until then.

A policy is Ready once accepted, wired and enforced by every engine:

  kubectl get wafpolicy -A
